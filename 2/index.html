Desensitization

Preparing your limb
Watch: the 2-minute routine
See exactly how firm, and where

Getting your limb ready to carry weight, so your socket feels like part of you, not a problem.

Right now the end of your limb is tender. That's normal. Before it can comfortably bear weight inside a socket, it needs to get used to being touched, pressed, and handled. That's desensitization.

The skin on your palms and soles is built for pressure, it's called acral skin. The end of your limb isn't. Desensitization teaches it to tolerate the job it's about to do.

The daily routine

A few minutes, a few times a day. Start gentle and build up: tapping, rubbing with different textures (a towel, then something coarser), and firm massage. It should be challenging, never painful.

Consistency beats intensity. Two minutes, often, does more than ten minutes once. This is one of the things only you can do to speed your progress.
